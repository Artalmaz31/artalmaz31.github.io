The first factor rewards sharp, confident peaks on certain prompts, and the second penalizes features that fire strongly but too rarely, likely artifacts of a few prompts. The 256 features of highest score per transcoder are shown, with the features the paper names or uses in its case studies, each with its five strongest prompts run again to record where it fires. The step profile of a feature is its mean activation over those five prompts at each step.
